Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 14, 2026

Fidget Spinner respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Fidget Spinner app.

Data Collection

Fidget Spinner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

The app does not use:

* Advertising or advertising SDKs
* Analytics or tracking services
* Third-party SDKs that collect user data
* User accounts or login systems
* Device identifiers for tracking
* Remote servers for storing user information

Motion Data

Fidget Spinner may access motion sensor data from your device, such as device orientation, gravity, and acceleration, to make the spinner’s physics, visual effects, sound, and haptic feedback respond naturally to device movement.

Motion data is processed in real time entirely on your device.

Motion data is not stored, uploaded, transmitted, shared, or used for tracking.

Local Preferences

The app stores a small number of preferences locally on your device, such as:

* Selected spinner material
* Glow setting
* Sound setting
* Haptic setting and haptic mode
* Motion effects setting
* Whether the introductory interaction guide has been completed

These preferences remain on your device and are not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Deleting the app removes these locally stored app preferences.

Device Permissions

Fidget Spinner does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or other personal content.

Device motion access is used only to provide the interactive spinner experience described above.

Children’s Privacy

Fidget Spinner does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal data, no personal information from children is stored by the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
