## 简体中文

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 20 日

Spinertia 尊重您的隐私。本隐私政策说明您使用 Spinertia App 时，相关信息如何被处理。

### 数据收集与共享

Spinertia 不收集、传输、出售或共享您的个人数据。

App 不使用：

- 广告或广告 SDK
- 分析统计或跟踪服务
- 收集用户数据的第三方 SDK
- 用户账户或登录系统
- 用于跟踪的设备标识符
- 用于存储用户信息的远程服务器

### 运动数据

Spinertia 可以访问设备姿态、重力和加速度等运动传感器数据，让陀螺的物理效果、视觉效果、声音和触感自然响应设备移动。

运动数据仅在您的设备上实时处理。运动数据不会被存储、上传、传输或共享，也不会用于跟踪。

您可以在 App 的“设置”中关闭“运动”。

### 本地偏好设置

App 会在您的设备本地保存少量偏好设置，例如：

- 所选陀螺材质
- 发光设置
- 声音设置
- 触感设置和触感模式
- 运动效果设置
- 是否已完成首次交互指引

这些偏好设置仅保留在您的设备上，不会传输给开发者或任何第三方。删除 App 会移除这些本地偏好设置。

### 设备权限

Spinertia 不会访问您的位置、相机、麦克风、照片、通讯录或其他个人内容。

设备运动权限仅用于上文所述的交互式陀螺体验。

### 离线使用

Spinertia 的核心体验可以完全离线运行。App 不需要账户，也不需要连接开发者服务器。

### 儿童隐私

Spinertia 不会有意收集儿童或其他用户的个人信息。由于 App 不收集或传输个人数据，开发者不会存储儿童的个人信息。

### 隐私政策变更

如果 App 功能或数据处理方式发生变化，本隐私政策可能会更新。更新内容会发布在本页面，并注明新的“最后更新”日期。

### 联系方式

如对本隐私政策或 Spinertia App 有疑问，请发送邮件至：

[gdni_support@163.com](mailto:gdni_support@163.com)

## English

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Spinertia respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Spinertia app.

### Data Collection and Sharing

Spinertia does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

The app does not use:

- Advertising or advertising SDKs
- Analytics or tracking services
- Third-party SDKs that collect user data
- User accounts or login systems
- Device identifiers for tracking
- Remote servers for storing user information

### Motion Data

Spinertia may access motion sensor data such as device orientation, gravity, and acceleration so the spinner’s physics, visual effects, sound, and haptic feedback can respond naturally to device movement.

Motion data is processed in real time only on your device. It is not stored, uploaded, transmitted, shared, or used for tracking.

You can turn Motion off in the app’s Settings.

### Local Preferences

The app stores a small number of preferences locally on your device, such as:

- Selected spinner material
- Glow setting
- Sound setting
- Haptic setting and haptic mode
- Motion effects setting
- Whether the introductory interaction guide has been completed

These preferences remain only on your device and are not transmitted to the developer or any third party. Deleting the app removes these locally stored preferences.

### Device Permissions

Spinertia does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or other personal content.

Device motion access is used only for the interactive spinner experience described above.

### Offline Use

Spinertia’s core experience works entirely offline. The app does not require an account or a connection to developer-operated servers.

### Children’s Privacy

Spinertia does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because the app does not collect or transmit personal data, no personal information from children is stored by the developer.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

### Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Spinertia app, email:
