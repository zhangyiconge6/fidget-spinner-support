Fidget Spinner Support

Thank you for using Fidget Spinner.

Fidget Spinner is a digital fidget toy designed to provide a realistic and relaxing spinning experience using physics, sound, haptic feedback, and device motion.

Contact Support

When reporting a problem, it is helpful to include:

* Your iPhone model
* Your iOS version
* A brief description of the issue
* The steps that caused the issue, if known

Please do not include passwords or other sensitive personal information in your email.

Privacy

Fidget Spinner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or third-party data collection SDKs.

Motion sensor data used for the spinner experience is processed locally on your device and is not stored or transmitted.

For more information, please see the Fidget Spinner Privacy Policy.

Common Questions

Does Fidget Spinner require an account?

No. The app does not require registration or login.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. The core Fidget Spinner experience works entirely on your device.

Why does the app use motion data?

Motion data allows the spinner’s physics and effects to respond naturally to movement of your iPhone. This information is processed on your device and is not uploaded.

How do I reset local app settings?

Deleting and reinstalling the app removes its locally stored preferences.
