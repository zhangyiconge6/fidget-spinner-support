## 简体中文

感谢使用 **Spinertia**。Spinertia 是一款数字指尖陀螺，通过物理反馈、声音、触感和设备运动带来轻松自然的旋转体验。

### 使用帮助

- 按住陀螺中心，可进行更稳定的握持旋转。
- 沿陀螺外圈拨动，可开始旋转或继续加速。
- 打开转速数据面板，可查看当前 RPM、最高速度和旋转时间。
- 在“设置”中可以选择材质，并分别控制声音、触感和运动效果。
- 选择“夜光绿”材质后，可以使用发光效果。
- Zen Mode 会隐藏界面提示，只保留陀螺。

### 联系支持

如果遇到问题、有使用疑问或希望提供反馈，请发送邮件至：

[gdni_support@163.com](mailto:gdni_support@163.com)

为了帮助我们定位问题，建议在邮件中注明：

- iPhone 型号
- iOS 版本
- 问题的简要描述
- 如果已知，触发问题的操作步骤

请勿在邮件中提供密码或其他敏感个人信息。

### 常见问题

#### Spinertia 需要账户吗？

不需要。App 无需注册或登录。

#### App 需要网络连接吗？

不需要。Spinertia 的核心体验可完全离线运行。

#### 为什么 App 使用运动数据？

开启“运动”后，Spinertia 会使用设备姿态、重力和加速度等运动数据，让陀螺自然响应 iPhone 的移动。数据仅在设备本地实时处理，不会上传。

#### 没有声音怎么办？

请确认“设置”中的“声音”已打开，并检查 iPhone 的媒体音量。

#### 没有触感怎么办？

请确认“设置”中的“触感”已打开。触感效果还取决于设备是否支持相应的触感功能。

#### 如何重置本地设置？

删除并重新安装 App 会移除保存在设备上的 Spinertia 偏好设置。

### 隐私

Spinertia 不收集、传输、出售或共享个人数据。App 不包含广告、分析统计、跟踪、账户系统或第三方数据收集 SDK。运动数据仅在设备本地处理，不会存储或上传。

## English

Thank you for using **Spinertia**. Spinertia is a digital fidget spinner designed to provide a natural, relaxing spinning experience through physics, sound, haptics, and device motion.

### Getting Started

- Hold the center for a steadier Grip Mode spin.
- Flick the outer edge to start spinning or add more speed.
- Open the spin stats panel to view current RPM, top speed, and spin time.
- Use Settings to choose a material and control Sound, Haptics, and Motion independently.
- Select Glow Green to use the Glow effect.
- Zen Mode hides interface hints and leaves only the spinner.

### Contact Support

If you experience a problem, have a question, or would like to provide feedback, email:

[gdni_support@163.com](mailto:gdni_support@163.com)

To help us investigate, please include:

- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- A brief description of the issue
- The steps that caused the issue, if known

Please do not include passwords or other sensitive personal information in your email.

### Common Questions

#### Does Spinertia require an account?

No. The app does not require registration or login.

#### Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Spinertia’s core experience works entirely offline.

#### Why does the app use motion data?

When Motion is enabled, Spinertia uses device orientation, gravity, and acceleration data so the spinner can respond naturally to movement of your iPhone. The data is processed in real time only on your device and is never uploaded.

#### What should I check if there is no sound?

Make sure Sound is enabled in Settings and check your iPhone’s media volume.

#### What should I check if there are no haptics?

Make sure Haptics is enabled in Settings. Haptic feedback also depends on device hardware support.

#### How do I reset local settings?

Deleting and reinstalling the app removes the Spinertia preferences stored on your device.

### Privacy

Spinertia does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system, or third-party data collection SDK. Motion data is processed only on your device and is not stored or uploaded.
